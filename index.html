Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 4, 2026

AppealCraft ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

AppealCraft does NOT collect, store, or share any personal data. All information you enter into the App remains on your device.

Local Storage

The App uses your device's local storage (UserDefaults) to save:

  • Appeal history (rejection text and generated responses)
  • Rejection patterns you create
  • App settings and preferences

This data never leaves your device. You can delete all stored data at any time by clearing the App's history or uninstalling the App.

Third-Party Services

The App communicates with a secure backend (Supabase Edge Function) only when generating AI-powered appeals. This service:

  • Receives only the rejection text you paste
  • Does NOT store your rejection text
  • Does NOT track or log user identity
  • Uses DeepSeek API to generate responses (no data stored)

No Accounts Required

AppealCraft does not require user accounts, logins, or registrations. We have no way to identify individual users.

No Analytics or Tracking

The App does not use analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or any form of usage tracking.

No Third-Party Sharing

Since we collect no data, we share no data with third parties.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for developers and professionals. It does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be posted in the App. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
